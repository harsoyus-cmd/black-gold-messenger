## 5. Usage Rules

1. Black is the dominant visual foundation.
2. Gold is an accent, not a replacement for the background.
3. Gold must not fill large areas unnecessarily.
4. Active navigation and important actions may use gold.
5. Status colors are reserved for their semantic meaning.
6. Text must remain highly readable against its background.
7. Avoid excessive gradients.
8. Avoid excessive glow effects.
9. Avoid game-like neon styling.
10. UI must remain lightweight for older Android devices.

## 6. BGM Visual Character

The visual direction is:

Luxury Technology

Not:
- Luxury jewelry
- Finance/corporate banking
- Gaming neon
- Industrial/block architecture

The interface should feel premium through restraint,
spacing, typography, contrast, and consistency.
