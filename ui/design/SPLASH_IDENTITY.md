# BGM Splash & Identity

Version: 0.1.0

## 1. Purpose

Define the initial visual identity shown when BGM starts.

## 2. Background

Primary background:
BGM_BLACK

The splash must remain visually simple and lightweight.

## 3. Logo

Brand:
Black Gold Messenger

Short name:
BGM

Logo:
Reserved for the final approved BGM logo.

The logo shape must not be invented or modified by the UI layer.

The final logo can be inserted later without redesigning
the splash structure.

## 4. Brand Text

Primary:
BLACK GOLD MESSENGER

Secondary:
BGM

No additional slogan is required at this stage.

## 5. Visual Direction

Style:
Luxury Technology

Characteristics:
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Calm
- Premium
- High contrast
- Recognizable

Avoid:
- Jewelry-style decoration
- Finance/corporate appearance
- Gaming neon
- Heavy 3D effects
- Excessive glow
- Excessive animation

## 6. Animation

Animation is optional.

If implemented:
- short
- subtle
- low CPU usage
- low battery impact

Never use:
- particle systems
- video backgrounds
- continuous effects
- heavy blur animation

## 7. Low-RAM Requirement

Splash must:
- load quickly
- use minimal memory
- avoid large assets
- avoid unnecessary dependencies
- release splash resources after startup

## 8. Future Compatibility

The splash structure must allow the final logo
to be inserted later without changing the overall layout.

The logo decision remains separate from the UI implementation.
